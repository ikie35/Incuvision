1. OBJECT DETECTION ARCHITECTURE & FRAMEWORK SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Model Architecture: YOLOv8 Nano (Ultralytics YOLOv8n-detect)
- Selection Rationale:
  * Computational Efficiency: ~3.2 Million parameters; lightweight footprint
    ideal for real-time edge execution on embedded ARM processors.
  * Anchor-Free Detection Head: Directly predicts bounding box centers and
    dimensions, providing superior generalization over varying egg shapes
    and tilted orientations in tray cups compared to older anchor-based models.
  * Task: Multi-Object Detection across the entire 56-egg incubator chamber frame
    (simultaneous localized bounding-box regression and fertility classification).

- Deployment Format:
  * Runtime Engine: Open Neural Network Exchange (ONNX) Runtime
  * Quantization/Precision: FP32 Static Tensor Shape (1 x 3 x 640 x 640)
  * ONNX Opset: Opset 12 (Universal compatibility standard for Linux ARM64 /
    Raspberry Pi OS Debian 12 without operator mismatch)
  * Edge Platform: Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB) utilizing CPU NEON/ARM SIMD optimization
  * Model File: yolov8ncandling.onnx (~12.7 MB)

2. DATASET COMPOSITION, ANNOTATION & DOMAIN ADAPTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Two-Stage Dataset Strategy:
   1. Stage 1 (Base Pre-training Dataset):
      - Sample Size: 653 multi-day candling images
      - Temporal Coverage: Incubation Days 3, 7, 10, 14, and 18
      - Purpose: Learn foundational biological development features:
        * Blood vessel branching (spider-vein network)
        * Embryo eye spot and embryonic movement mass
        * Air cell expansion and fluid opacity gradients
      - Resulting Pre-trained Weights: yolov8n_candling.pt

   2. Stage 2 (In-Situ Domain Adaptation Dataset):
      - Sample Size: 20 full-frame images captured directly inside the IncuVision
        chamber by the overhead Raspberry Pi Camera Module.
      - Annotation Platform: Roboflow
        * Workspace: "ike-russel"
        * Project: "finetune-skerg" (Version 2)
      - Total Labeled Instances: ~1,100 individual egg annotations across the
        56-cup kinematic turning tray grid.
      - Purpose: Adapt detection heads to the specific physical lighting, LED
        candling wavelength, lens distortion, and optical angles of the
        IncuVision chamber.

B. Class Ontology (Binary Classification):
   - Class 0: 'fertile'
     * Visual Criteria: Clear network of branching blood vessels, visible dark
       embryo body, spider-like vascular structures, or dark opaque development
       (Day 7+).
   - Class 1: 'infertile'
     * Visual Criteria: Clear translucent appearance, uniform bright yellow/orange
       illumination, floating unorganized yolk shadow, blood rings (early dead),
       or lack of vascularization.

C. Image Pre-processing (Roboflow):
   - Auto-Orientation: Applied (strips camera EXIF rotation tags)
   - Resize: Static 640 x 640 with letterbox black padding (preserves 1:1 aspect
     ratio of round eggs without elliptical distortion)

5. INFERENCE PIPELINE & EDGE OPTIMIZATIONS (RASPBERRY PI 5)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. ONNX Runtime Execution:
   - Pre-processing:
     * Frame acquired via OpenCV / Picamera2 (1920x1080 BGR)
     * Resized to 640x640: cv2.dnn.blobFromImage(resized, 1/255.0, swapRB=True)
   - Inference Output Tensor:
     * Dimensions: (1, 6, 8400) -> Transposed to (8400, 6)
     * Format: [cx, cy, width, height, p_fertile, p_infertile]
   - Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) Calibrated Thresholds:
     * CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD = 0.15
       (Optimized from default 0.45 down to 0.15 to reliably capture dimmer
        peripheral and corner eggs subject to camera lens vignetting).
     * IOU_THRESHOLD = 0.60
       (Tuned from 0.50 to 0.60 to prevent NMS from suppressing closely packed
        neighboring eggs in the 8x7 tray cups).
   - Inference Latency on Raspberry Pi 5:
     * Average inference latency: ~110 - 145 ms per full-chamber scan.

B. Spatial Sorting & Indexing Algorithm:
   - After NMS filtering, detected eggs are dynamically sorted top-to-bottom,
     left-to-right into human-readable tray reading order:
     * Clustering formula: key = (round(center_y / 60.0) * 60.0, center_x)
     * Eggs are labeled #1 through #56 with their corresponding Row (1-8)
       and Column (1-7) assignment for individual egg tracking over 21 days.

C. Physical Seating & Optical Insights for Candling:
   - Egg Orientation: Eggs positioned blunt-end (air cell) UP and small-end DOWN
     resting directly over the sub-tray LED aperture maximize light transmission
     through the egg yolk and embryo, eliminating dark cup-shadow false negatives.
